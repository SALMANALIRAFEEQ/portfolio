## Why Live Chat Improves Conversions  
A few key points explain why adding live chat can lift your conversion rate:

- **Instant answers** – A visitor who sees a question answered right away is more likely to buy.
- **Human touch** – Even a short chat can make a site feel personal, especially for small businesses that want a friendly vibe.
- **Lead capture** – Chats can collect email addresses or phone numbers before a customer decides to shop.
- **Reduced support cost** – By answering simple queries online you free up time for other tasks.

If you’re running a small Shopify shop, a live‑chat bot or a lightweight app can give you most of these benefits without a big investment.

## Step‑by‑Step Install Guide  
Below is a practical walk‑through for adding Tidio to your Shopify store. The process is the same for any other app, just replace the app name.

1. **Install the app**  
   - Go to the [Shopify App Store](https://www.shopify.com/) and search for *Tidio*.  
   - Click **Add app**, then **Install app** on the confirmation page.

2. **Create a Tidio account**  
   - After installation you’ll be redirected to Tidio’s website.  
   - Sign up with your store’s email.  
   - Choose the *Free* plan if you want to keep costs low.

3. **Connect to Shopify**  
   - In the Tidio dashboard, click **Integrations** > **Shopify**.  
   - Authorise the connection; Tidio will read your product catalogue automatically.

4. **Add the chat widget**  
   - In Tidio’s **Widgets** section, click **Add new widget**.  
   - Pick the *Standard* layout and click **Save**.  
   - The app injects a small script tag into your theme automatically, so you don’t need to touch any code.

5. **Publish changes**  
   - In Shopify, go to **Online Store** > **Themes**.  
   - Click **Actions** > **Edit code** for the active theme.  
   - If the script isn’t present, paste the snippet from Tidio’s **Settings** > **Install** tab before `</body>` and click **Save**.

Your chat should now appear on every page. Open a browser window and reload the site to see it in action.

## Personalising the Widget  
A generic chat icon can feel impersonal. Customising a few settings makes the experience feel more like your brand.

### Colours and placement
- In Tidio’s **Appearance** settings you can change the bubble’s colour to match your logo.  
- Move the chat icon to the bottom‑right corner (default) or top‑right if that suits your layout better.

### Greeting message
- Set a short, friendly greeting such as “Hi! Need help finding the right product?”  
- You can schedule different messages for weekdays and weekends.

### Automated triggers
- Use the **Triggers** tab to show a chat window when a visitor scrolls near the bottom of the page.  
- For new visitors, display a welcome message that offers a discount code in exchange for a chat.

### Language support
- If you sell internationally, add a language switcher in the widget so visitors can choose their preferred language.

## Measuring Chat Success  
Adding a chat is only useful if you can see whether it’s working. Track these simple metrics:

| Metric | Why it matters | How to check |
|---|---|---|
| **Chat volume** | Shows how many visitors are reaching out | Tidio dashboard > Chats |
| **Average response time** | Faster replies lead to higher satisfaction | Tidio > Analytics |
| **Conversion rate from chat** | Direct link between chat and sales | Tidio > Reports (link to order IDs) |
| **Chat abandonment** | If many chats are closed without reply, tweak greeting or triggers | Tidio > Insights |

> If your chat volume is low, try changing the trigger or adding a pop‑up message that invites visitors to start a conversation.

## Frequently asked questions {#faq data-toc-label="FAQ"}

### Can I use live chat if I don’t have a lot of staff?
Yes. Most Shopify live‑chat apps let you set up automated responses or a simple “I’m away” message. You can reply to a few key questions manually or schedule a time to check the inbox.

### Does live chat slow down my website?
A lightweight widget adds only a few kilobytes of code. If you’re already using a fast theme and keep your app list minimal, the impact on load time will be negligible.

### Will live chat help me get more reviews?
Not directly, but a quick, helpful conversation can encourage a buyer to leave a positive review. Use a follow‑up message after purchase to ask for feedback.
